What shape should the broker map take?

Same data in all three: ~500 brokers, a few bright hubs, a faint crowd. Lime = you filed. Amber = re-check due. Magenta ✕ = no opt-out exists. Dim = not yet. (Fake positions and links, just to show the shape.)

A

Constellation

A star map. Hubs are bright stars, and the crowd forms a nebula around the hubs it buys from. Filing an opt-out lights a star. It feels the most like a night sky going dark-to-lit, but you don't see a clear direction of flow.

B

Supply-chain river

Scout's sentence drawn literally: data flows left to right, from where it's collected, through the wholesalers, out to the retail resellers and the people who buy it. You see exactly where you "cut the chain." It reads clearest, but looks more like a diagram than a place.

C

The Shadow City

The brokers are a city too, the one underneath yours. Hubs are towers and the crowd is a sprawl of small buildings, with pipes running between them. Filing an opt-out takes a building's lights out (or reclaims it). It matches the game's look, but the links are harder to read.
